# Canon — where this comes from, with sources

**Read when:** a claim in this skill is challenged, or you need to argue a
position rather than assert it.

Every persona in `personas/` is a composite. The lineage below is real, and this
file exists so the claims in this skill can be **argued with rather than taken on
vibes**.

---

**Dieter Rams** — *good design is as little design as possible*, and the ten
principles — and the **Swiss tradition** (Müller-Brockmann, Ruder) that made the
grid, the scale and the measure into an argument rather than a preference.
Everything in `craft/space.md` and `craft/type.md` about ratios, measure and
rhythm descends from here, not from any operating system.

**Apple's clarity, deference and depth** — legible text at any size, minimal
ornament, the interface receding so the content leads, hierarchy carried by
layering and space rather than decoration. The reasoning transfers to any
surface; the material of the moment does not.
[HIG](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines) ·
[Liquid Glass, WWDC25](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/219/) ·
`surface/apple.md`

**WCAG 2.2** — the contrast, focus and target-size numbers in this skill are AA
criteria, not preferences. 2.5.8 Target Size (Minimum) is 24×24 CSS px; 2.4.11
Focus Not Obscured is AA; the 4.5:1 and 3:1 floors are 1.4.3 and 1.4.11.
[W3C](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html)

**Nielsen's ten usability heuristics** (1994, still the working checklist) and
**Norman's *Design of Everyday Things*** — affordance, feedback, mapping,
constraint. Levels 0 and 5 of `process/review.md` are these two, applied.
Norman's three levels — visceral, behavioural, reflective — are the time
horizons in `flow/first-run.md`.
[NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/)

**Gestalt principles** — proximity, similarity, closure, continuity. Proximity
before line, box or tint (`craft/space.md`) is the first of these, applied
literally.

**Laws of UX** (Yablonski) — Fitts, Hick, Jakob, and the Doherty threshold's
400ms, from Doherty & Thadani, *IBM Systems Journal*, 1982.
[lawsofux.com](https://lawsofux.com/doherty-threshold/)

**Rauno Freiberg, *Invisible Details of Interaction Design*** — interruptible
animations, momentum carried out of a gesture, animation budget by frequency,
magic corners as infinite Fitts targets, feedback before commitment.
[rauno.me](https://rauno.me/craft/interaction-design)

**Julie Zhuo, *The Making of a Manager*** — a great designer defends work on
principles that last. The source of "taste is debuggable" in
`core/beliefs.md`.

**John Maeda** — *subtract the obvious, add the meaningful*.

**Jony Ive** — people can sense care and can sense carelessness; different and
new is relatively easy, genuinely better is very hard. `voice/commitment.md`.

**Joe Gebbia** — designing for trust between strangers, and storyboarding the
emotional journey before the pixels (the "Snow White" method).
`process/plan-review.md` pass 3.

**Ira Glass** — your taste is why your work disappoints you. The gap is the
job, not a defect in you.

**The generic-default tells** are documented, not invented: purple-blue
gradients, reflex dark mode, glassmorphism over pastel blobs, 3D figures with
orbs, default Inter, and the 3-column icon-circle feature grid are the current
recognisable signature of unconsidered machine output.
[OpenAI, *Designing Delightful Frontends*
(2026)](https://developers.openai.com/blog/designing-delightful-frontends-with-gpt-5-4) ·
[UX Collective](https://uxdesign.cc/ai-design-isnt-ugly-it-s-fluent-and-that-s-the-problem-131b2f4eb78c)

**The terminal tradition** — VT100/ANSI escape sequences, ncurses, and the
working vocabulary of vim, tmux, mc and htop; Charm's Bubble Tea and Lip Gloss,
and ratatui, for the modern layout-in-cells model. `surface/tui.md`.
