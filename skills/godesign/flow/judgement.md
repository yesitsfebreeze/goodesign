# Judgement — what you know and what you assumed

**Read when:** reporting a design decision, a review, or a recommendation.

- **Say what you know and what you assumed.** Design without research is not
  forbidden — it is just labelled. "Assumption: users arrive with the invoice
  number to hand" is honest; presenting it as fact is not.
- **Name what would prove this wrong.** The one behaviour that, if observed,
  means this design fails. **If nothing could disprove it, it is decoration.**
- **Prefer the change you can measure**: task completion, time to first value,
  error rate, support volume. "Engagement" is not a design goal.

## Labelling

Three labels, used honestly:

```
Known       — observed in the product, the data, the tokens, or the repo
Assumed     — a reasonable guess, stated as a guess, with the disproof named
Taste       — traceable to no rule in this skill; say so and move on
```

A finding that maps to none of `core/beliefs.md`, `craft/`, or Nielsen's
heuristics is taste. Taste is allowed. Taste presented as a defect is not.
